Optical models for remote sensing of chromophoric dissolved organic matter (CDOM) absorption in Poyang Lake
Xu et al., ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, 2018

Abstract & Objectives
CDOM is a key aquatic component, affecting biogeochemical cycles, primary production, and UV protection.
The study develops and validates models for estimating CDOM absorption at 355 nm (ag(355)) in Poyang Lake, China, using in situ reflectance and water samples.
Models are tailored for clean (TSM < 10 mg/L) and turbid (TSM ≥ 10 mg/L) water, using band ratios from field and simulated satellite data (Sentinel-2 MSI, Landsat-8 OLI).
Objectives:
Characterize CDOM absorption and water reflectance spectra to identify sensitive band ratios for remote sensing.
Establish and evaluate algorithms using simulated Sentinel-2 and Landsat-8 bands for CDOM estimation.
Assess the potential of these algorithms for CDOM retrieval in floodplain lakes and wetlands.
Study Area & Sampling
Poyang Lake: Largest freshwater lake in China, complex hydrology, significant seasonal variation, affected by both natural and anthropogenic factors.
Sampling: 92 samples from four areas (A–D) during 12 cruises (Aug 2015–Jul 2016). Surface water (0–50 cm) collected, with in situ reflectance measured (350–2500 nm, 1 nm interval) using Fieldspec 4 spectroradiometer.
Water quality parameters measured: CDOM absorption (ag(355), ag(400), ag(440)), total suspended matter (TSM), chlorophyll-a (Chl-a), organic/inorganic suspended matter (OSM/ISM).
Methods
Field Spectroscopy & Water Quality
Reflectance measurement: Above-water, with corrections for sky and reference panel radiance.
Remote sensing reflectance (Rrs):
Rrs = Lw / Ed(0+)
Lw = water-leaving radiance
Ed(0+) = downwelling irradiance
CDOM absorption coefficient:
a'_g(lambda) = 2.303 * D(lambda) / l
a_g(lambda) = a'_g(lambda) - a'_g(700)
D(lambda) = optical density
l = path length (0.1 m in this study)
Spectral slope of CDOM:
a_g(lambda) = a_g(lambda0) * exp[-Sg * (lambda - lambda0)]
lambda0 = 440 nm
Sg = fitted exponential decay parameter
Satellite Band Simulation
Sentinel-2 MSI: 13 bands (10–60 m resolution), atmospheric correction with Sen2Cor to obtain BOA reflectance.
Band simulation:
Rb(i) = integral from lambda_min to lambda_max of [SRF(lambda) * Rrs(lambda) d(lambda)] divided by integral from lambda_min to lambda_max of [SRF(lambda) d(lambda)]
SRF(lambda) = sensor’s spectral response function
Model Development
Band ratio models: Empirical relationships between band ratios and ag(355), developed separately for clean and turbid water.
Model calibration/validation: 2/3 of samples for calibration, 1/3 for validation, using r², RMSE, and %RMSE as metrics.
Key Results
Water Quality
ag(355): Mean 2.31 ± 0.40 m⁻¹ (range: 1.70–3.33 m⁻¹)
TSM: Mean 31.2 ± 26.1 mg/L (range: 3.6–114 mg/L)
Chl-a: Mean 5.66 ± 4.88 μg/L (range: 0.37–21.9 μg/L)
Spectral slopes: S275-295, S350-400, S400-600 all around 0.016 nm⁻¹
Model Performance (Field Data)
Best band ratios:
Clean water: R(689)/R(497), r = 0.799, r² = 0.70
Turbid water: R(767)/R(826), r = 0.802, r² = 0.73
Validation:
Clean water: %RMSE = 13.2%
Turbid water: %RMSE = 11.6%
Model Performance (Simulated Satellite Bands)
Sentinel-2 MSI:
Clean water: B4/B2 or B5/B2 (best: B4/B2, r² = 0.70, RMSE = 0.24 m⁻¹, %RMSE = 9.5%)
Turbid water: B7/B8 or B7/B8A (best: B7/B8, r² = 0.34, RMSE = 0.29 m⁻¹, %RMSE = 12.8%)
Landsat-8 OLI:
Clean water: B4/B2 (r² = 0.62)
Turbid water: B4/B5 (r² = 0.13, much lower accuracy than Sentinel-2)
Validation (Sentinel-2):
Clean water: B5/B2, r² = 0.70, RMSE = 0.31 m⁻¹, %RMSE = 12.5%
Turbid water: B7/B8, r² = 0.45, RMSE = 0.26 m⁻¹, %RMSE = 11.7%
Application Example
Sentinel-2 image (July 26, 2016): Used to map TSM and ag(355) spatially.
TSM retrieval: Single-band B7 algorithm, r² = 0.81, RMSE = 5.72 mg/L, %RMSE = 27.7%
CDOM retrieval: Band ratio models applied after separating clean/turbid water by TSM.
Discussion & Insights
CDOM retrieval in turbid inland waters is challenging due to overlapping absorption by Chl-a and TSM.
Band ratio models that distinguish between clean and turbid water improve accuracy.
ag(355) is the most sensitive and reliable absorption coefficient for CDOM remote sensing in this context.
Sentinel-2 outperforms Landsat-8 for CDOM retrieval, especially in turbid water, due to additional narrow bands in the red/NIR and higher spatial resolution.
Correlation analysis: In turbid water, CDOM is negatively correlated with TSM and OSM, positively with Chl-a.
Spectral features: The NIR reflectance peak (~810 nm) is influenced by pure water absorption and high scattering particles.

Conclusions
Band ratio models (R(689)/R(497) for clean, R(767)/R(826) for turbid) are effective for estimating ag(355) in Poyang Lake.
Sentinel-2 MSI is recommended for CDOM monitoring in complex floodplain lakes due to better performance and spatial resolution.
The study provides a foundation for spatial and temporal monitoring of CDOM using remote sensing in large, optically complex lakes.
